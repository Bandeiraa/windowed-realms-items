Windowed Realms — Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 17, 2026

This policy explains which personal data the game Windowed Realms (the "Game") uses, why, and what your rights are, under Brazil's General Data Protection Law (LGPD, Law 13,709/2018) and other applicable privacy laws, such as the GDPR for players in the European Union.

Controller: Tiny Loom Studio — tinyloomstudio@gmail.com

1. Summary

  • The Game has no server of its own, no ads, no tracking and no usage analytics.
  • We do not ask for your email, address, ID documents or payment data. Purchases and payments happen on Steam, through Valve.
  • Online features use Steam. Bug reports and suggestions go to a Google Form, only when you send them.

2. Data the Game uses

Through Steam (Valve): - Your Steam ID, profile name and profile picture, to show you on the leaderboard and in PvP. - Leaderboard scores with the run details: room reached, fight time, the party used (can be hidden in the Game), and the chosen frame and title. - Unlocked achievements and game status (what you are doing, shown to your Steam friends). - Items in your Steam inventory (appearances) and the date you got the Game license, used to give the titles of players who played before launch. - In PvP, matchmaking uses Steam lobbies and the match connects the two players through Steam's network. We do not store its content or the connection address.

Bug reports and suggestions (only if you send one): - The name you type (optional), the type (bug or suggestion) and the message. - Technical information: Game version, language, operating system and the date and time it was sent. - This data goes to a Google Form and is kept in a spreadsheet that only the studio can access. Until it is sent, the report stays on your computer.

On your computer: save files, settings and technical logs stay only on your computer and are not sent to us.

3. Why we use it and the legal basis

  • Online features (leaderboard, PvP, achievements, appearances): to provide what the Game offers you (performance of a contract).
  • Reports: to fix errors and improve the Game, based on your voluntary submission (consent) and our legitimate interest in keeping the Game working.
  • Leaderboard protection: to identify and remove results obtained by cheating (legitimate interest).

We do not sell your data and do not use it for advertising.

4. Who we share it with

  • Valve (Steam): runs the online features and also processes your data under Valve's own privacy policy.
  • Google (Forms and Sheets): receives and stores the reports.

These services may store data outside your country, such as in the United States. These transfers are needed for the Game to work and are made by companies with data protection safeguards.

5. How long we keep it

  • Leaderboards and achievements: while they exist on Steam. Leaderboards from old versions may be deleted.
  • Reports: up to 24 months after they are sent, or less if no longer needed.
  • Data on your computer: until you delete it.

6. Your rights

You can ask for: confirmation that we process your data, access, correction, anonymization or deletion, information about sharing, and withdrawal of consent. Write to tinyloomstudio@gmail.com. We will reply within 15 days.

In the Game you can also hide your party on the leaderboard or hide yourself from the leaderboard. Your Steam account data is managed by Valve, in Steam's privacy settings.

If you are not satisfied, you can complain to the Brazilian National Data Protection Authority (ANPD) or to the data protection authority of your country.

7. Children

The Game is not directed to children and does not collect data beyond what is described here. Steam accounts follow Valve's minimum age. Parents or guardians can ask for the deletion of a report sent by a minor at the email above.

8. Security

We rely on the protections of the services above and restrict access to reports to the studio. No system is completely secure. If an incident could put you at risk, we will notify you as required by law.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy. The new version is published at this address, with the date at the top.
